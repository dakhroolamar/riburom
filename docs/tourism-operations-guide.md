# Daily tourism operations

## Start the day

Open Overview, then use **My work**, **Due today** and **Overdue** to plan follow-ups. Due today and Overdue cover the team's open work. Use **My dashboard preferences** to choose the activity you see; this does not change organisation email or messaging delivery.

## Work on an enquiry

1. Open Enquiries and select a traveller. On a phone, the detail screen opens directly; use Back to enquiries to return.
2. Accept & assign to me, or choose an active staff member in the work update.
3. Contact the traveller using Email or WhatsApp when a suitable phone number is available. These links open your communication app; they do not record or send messages through the CMS.
4. Set the status, next action and follow-up date. Add a progress note and save the work update.
5. Use Mark as spam for irrelevant solicitations. Spam stays available in the Spam view and can be reopened. For several records, select their checkboxes and apply a bulk action after reviewing the selection.

## Update or create a package

Use **New Package** on Overview or **Packages** in Marketplace. Edit a package or duplicate it as a starting point for a new itinerary. Duplication copies text and pricing; upload the new cover and itinerary PDF separately.

Set nights/days and choose a starting price in INR with a per-person or per-group basis. Use Price on request when no starting price is advertised. Existing custom price and duration text can be retained. Enter one itinerary day per line, or use Add day to append a day. Check the inclusions, exclusions, current cover and PDF.

The save bar stays available while editing. **Preview saved package** shows the last saved version, including an unpublished package; it does not show unsaved typing. Saving with Published checked updates the live website. Clear Published to keep the saved package private. Changing the title keeps its existing public URL. Packages linked to a quote or trip must be unpublished rather than deleted.

## Prepare a quote and operational trip

Closed, declined and spam enquiries must be reopened before preparing a quote or converting to a trip. Revising a quote for a confirmed enquiry keeps it confirmed.

From an enquiry, choose Create Quote and optionally select a package. Use package details to prefill the itinerary and inclusions, then confirm the final traveller/group total and quote validity date. The package starting price is not automatically used as the group total. Save and open the printable quote from Documents, then share through your usual communication channel.

Use Convert to Trip to retain the enquiry link. Select a package and apply its destination/itinerary, then set the actual travel dates, traveller count and assignments. Approved partner names are suggested where their types match; manual entry remains available. A previous trip can also supply itinerary and assignments. Neither copying method reuses old departure dates or confirms partner availability.

## Maintain partners

Use Partners to add or edit a partner. Keep the contact person, base location and service area current. Operational notes are private to the management workflow. Upload verification documents and review their expiry dates in the partner list.

Draft, awaiting verification, under review and documents verified describe review progress. Only APPROVED, PUBLISHED or ACTIVE partners with Enabled checked appear publicly. Enabled does not mean a partner is available for a particular booking date; confirm the booking with them.

## Update website and media

Website page text follows **Save Draft → Preview saved draft → Publish Saved Draft**. Review the saved change summary before publishing. Images and appearance use immediate saves and are labelled as live changes. Media titles, alt text and captions are shared wherever that image is used.

Give photographs descriptive titles and location/tags so colleagues can find them. Use the media usage links to reach their page gallery. Rotate an incorrectly oriented source before replacing it; use the gallery's focal controls for cropping. Replacing a shared photograph affects its other uses.

## Team access

An Owner can apply the **Tourism Operations** preset when creating or editing a team member. It covers packages, enquiries, trips, media and partners, including their management actions. It does not grant user administration or page-text publishing. Owners can adjust the permission checkboxes before saving. Media permission allows immediate changes to public images, so assign it deliberately to staff responsible for those updates.

## Editing alongside colleagues

Packages, trips, checklists and enquiry actions reject a save when someone else has changed that record since you opened it. On a conflict, reload, review the latest saved version and reapply your intended changes. This prevents an older form from replacing a colleague's work.
