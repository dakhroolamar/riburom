How It Works

Your journey to Meghalaya, simplified into six seamless steps

1

Discover & Enquire

Browse our curated experiences, pick your adventure, and send us an enquiry or book a call at your preferred time — no obligation. We'll talk through the experiences, your ideal dates, and review availability.

2

Confirm Your Dates

Once you confirm, a deposit secures your spot. We'll guide you through the necessary forms — travel insurance and a medical check (within 30 days) — to ensure your safety and comfort.

3

Plan Your Journey

Book flexible flights to Guwahati/Shillong. Arrive 1–2 days before your trip. We can arrange accommodation, private transfers, and curated experiences in Shillong or the surrounding hills.

4

Preparation & Packing

As departure approaches, we'll guide you through what to pack. Purchase recommended gear through our Boutique or have our team prepare a full kit ready for your arrival.

5

Safety Briefing

On arrival, our team will meet you for a detailed briefing — itinerary, weather updates, safety protocols. We'll check your kit, assist with anything missing, and ensure you're fully prepared.

6

Depart for Adventure

Our drivers escort you to your journey's starting point. From there, your handcrafted Meghalaya experience begins — landscapes, culture, and connections await.
